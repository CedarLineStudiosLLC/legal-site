Part A — Applies to all Cedar Line Studios apps

1. Who we are

Cedar Line Studios LLC ("Cedar Line Studios," "we," "us") is a U.S. company based in Washington State that develops and publishes mobile games and utility apps (the "Apps"). This policy explains what data the Apps collect, how it is used, who it is shared with, and the choices and rights you have.

Contact: contact@cedarlinestudios.com

The short version: the Apps do not require an account, do not ask for your name, email address, or contact details, do not show third-party ads, do not track you across other companies' apps or websites, and do not sell your personal data. We collect a limited amount of pseudonymous usage, device, and crash data so we can keep the Apps working and improve them. Content you create in an App is stored on your device and, where noted, in your own iCloud account — not on our servers.

3. Data we collect (all apps)

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, or other directly identifying information through the Apps, and the Apps have no account or sign-up system.

3.1 Usage and analytics data

We use Google Firebase Analytics to understand how the Apps are used. This includes:

  • Screens viewed and in-app events (e.g., taps, game actions, feature usage)
  • A random app-instance identifier generated by Firebase for your installation of the App (this identifier is not tied to your name or Apple ID, and resets if you reinstall the App)
  • Device and technical information automatically collected by Firebase, such as device model, operating system version, app version, language/region, and approximate (city-level) location derived from your IP address

Analytics collection is enabled by default and begins when you launch the App. We use this data only in aggregate to understand feature usage; we do not use it to identify you personally, to build advertising profiles, or to track you across other companies' apps or websites.

3.2 Crash and diagnostic data

We use Google Firebase Crashlytics to detect and fix crashes and errors. When the App crashes or encounters an error, Crashlytics collects:

  • Crash stack traces and error reports
  • Device state and technical information at the time of the error (e.g., device model, OS version, available memory)
  • A random installation identifier

3.3 Data stored on your device

The Apps store data locally on your device (for example, game progress, scores, settings, and preferences). This data stays on your device unless it is synced through a service described below, and we cannot access it.

3.4 Game Center and iCloud (apps that use them — see Part B)

For Apps that integrate Apple Game Center, we use it for leaderboards, achievements, and saved game data. Game Center may process your Game Center player identifier, alias/nickname, avatar, scores, and friend relationships. Leaderboards may display the public aliases and avatars of other players. Saved game data may be synced to your personal iCloud account via Apple CloudKit; that data lives in your Apple account, not on Cedar Line Studios servers. Game Center and iCloud are operated by Apple and governed by Apple's Privacy Policy. You can manage Game Center (including your alias and who can see your profile) and iCloud in your device Settings.

3.5 When you contact us

The Apps include a "Contact us" option that opens your own email app; nothing is sent unless you choose to send it. If you email us, we receive your email address, anything you include in the message, and any information your email provider attaches. We use this information only to respond to you and to improve the Apps, we do not add you to any marketing list, and we do not share it with anyone except as described in §5. We keep support emails only as long as needed to handle your request and for a reasonable period afterward for record-keeping, then delete them.

3.6 What we do NOT collect

To be explicit, the Apps do not:

  • Require or support user accounts, or collect names, emails, or passwords
  • Access your contacts, photos, camera, microphone, or health data
  • Use the device advertising identifier (IDFA) or any third-party advertising SDK, or engage in "tracking" as defined by Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework
  • Collect precise (GPS) location on our servers (see Part B for how MealMap handles location entirely on-device and in your own iCloud)

4. How we use data, and our legal bases

We use the data described above:

  • To operate, maintain, and improve the Apps
  • To diagnose and fix crashes and technical problems
  • To understand feature usage and improve gameplay and design
  • To provide leaderboards, saved games, and sync features (where applicable)
  • To respond when you contact us
  • To comply with law and to protect our legal rights

Where laws such as the EU/UK GDPR apply, we rely on the following legal bases: our legitimate interests in operating, securing, and improving the Apps (analytics and crash reporting); your consent, where required by local law, which you may withdraw at any time (see §8); and compliance with legal obligations. Where local law requires consent for analytics identifiers, you can withhold or withdraw it using the controls in §8.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not "share" it for cross-context behavioral advertising (as those terms are defined in the California Consumer Privacy Act). We have not done so in the preceding 12 months. We do not use your personal data for targeted advertising or profiling that produces legal or similarly significant effects, and we do not knowingly collect or process sensitive personal data.

5. Who we share data with (processors)

We share data only with service providers that process it on our behalf to provide the functions described above, and with authorities where required by law (for example, to comply with a lawful request, or to protect the rights, safety, or property of users, the public, or Cedar Line Studios). If Cedar Line Studios is ever involved in a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, data may be transferred as part of that transaction; this policy would continue to apply to it until updated.

6. International data transfers

Cedar Line Studios is based in the United States, and the service providers above process data on servers that may be located in the United States or other countries. Where data is transferred out of the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, our providers rely on recognized transfer mechanisms — Google LLC and Apple Inc. participate in the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework (and its UK and Swiss extensions) and/or use Standard Contractual Clauses.

7. Data retention

  • Analytics data: user-level analytics data (including the app-instance identifier) is retained by Firebase for a limited period (by default, up to 14 months), after which only aggregate reporting data remains.
  • Crash data: Crashlytics retains crash reports for a limited period (currently 90 days by default).
  • Support emails: kept only as long as needed (see §3.5).
  • Data on your device: persists until you delete the App or clear its data.
  • Data in your iCloud account: persists in your Apple account until you delete it in the App or via iCloud settings; we do not control or have access to your iCloud data.

We do not retain personal data indefinitely, and we do not retain it longer than reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy.

8. Your choices and controls

Regardless of where you live, you can:

  • Limit analytics and crash reporting through your device's privacy settings (Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements, and per-app settings where offered).
  • Manage Game Center (alias, avatar, visibility) and iCloud data in your device Settings and at privacy.apple.com.
  • Deny or revoke device permissions (such as location, where an App requests it) at any time in Settings without losing access to the rest of the App.
  • Delete all locally stored data at any time by deleting the App, and delete synced data using in-app delete options (see Part B) or iCloud settings.

9. Your privacy rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or receive a portable copy of your personal data; to restrict or object to certain processing; to withdraw consent; and to opt out of sale, sharing, targeted advertising, or profiling (we do not engage in any of those). We will not discriminate against you for exercising any of these rights.

To exercise a right, email contact@cedarlinestudios.com with the App name and your request. We will respond within the time required by applicable law (generally 30–45 days). Where permitted, an authorized agent may submit a request on your behalf. If we deny a request, you may appeal by replying to our response; if the appeal is denied, you may contact your state Attorney General or, in the EEA/UK, lodge a complaint with your local data protection supervisory authority.

An important honest limitation: because the Apps have no accounts and the identifiers we hold are random per-installation values, we usually cannot link analytics or crash data to you specifically. Under laws such as GDPR Article 11, where we cannot identify you in our data, some rights (like access or deletion of analytics records) may not be technically fulfillable; in that case the most effective controls are the device-level ones in §8, deleting the App (which resets the analytics identifier), and managing your Game Center/iCloud data directly with Apple. Content you create in an App is under your direct control — you can view, edit, and delete it in the App itself.

10. California disclosures

For California residents, in the preceding 12 months we have collected these categories of personal information as defined by the CCPA/CPRA: identifiers (random app-instance and installation identifiers; your email address only if you email us); internet or other electronic network activity information (in-app usage events, device and crash diagnostics); and coarse geolocation (city-level, derived from IP address by our analytics provider). We collect them for the purposes in §4, share them only with the processors in §5, retain them as described in §7, and do not sell them or share them for cross-context behavioral advertising. We do not collect sensitive personal information. California residents may exercise the rights in §9, including through an authorized agent.

11. Security

We rely on the platform security of iOS and on the security programs of Apple and Google for data in transit and at rest, and we limit the data we collect in the first place (data we never collect cannot be breached). No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. If a breach affecting your personal data occurs, we will notify you and regulators as required by law.

12. Children's privacy

The Apps are not directed to children under 13 (or the higher minimum age required in your jurisdiction — for example, 16 in some EEA countries), and they are age-rated accordingly on the App Store. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13, and we do not use verifiable parental consent mechanisms because the Apps are not intended for children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has used the Apps or provided us personal information (for example, by emailing us), contact contact@cedarlinestudios.com and we will delete it promptly.

13. If we add ads, purchases, or accounts in the future

Today the Apps contain no third-party advertising, no in-app purchases, and no accounts. If we add any of these to an App, we will update this policy and the App's App Store privacy label before the feature launches, describe exactly what changes (including any new providers in §5 and Part B), and, where required by law or by Apple (for example, the App Tracking Transparency prompt), ask for your permission before any new collection begins.

We may update this policy from time to time. The current version will always be available at this address, and the "Last updated" date will reflect the latest revision. MealMap

In addition to the data described in Part A, MealMap collects and processes:

Location data

MealMap uses your device's location to find and log nearby venues. With your permission, we access your precise location while you are using the App. Location is also stored as part of the venues you save (address, latitude, and longitude of the venue).

Your precise (GPS) location is processed only on your device — for example, to sort venues by distance — and is synced solely to your private iCloud account as part of the venues you save. Cedar Line Studios does not transmit your precise location, or the latitude and longitude of your saved venues, to our own servers or to analytics. The only third party that receives location is Apple, through Maps/MapKit and iCloud (see below). Separately, as noted in Part A §3.1, Firebase Analytics infers an approximate, city-level location from your IP address; this is not your device's GPS location.

You can grant or revoke location access at any time in your device Settings. Denying location access limits location-dependent features but does not prevent you from using the rest of the App.

Maps

MealMap displays maps and venue information using Apple Maps / MapKit. Your location and search context may be processed by the map provider to render maps and search results, subject to Apple's Privacy Policy.

Usage analytics specific to MealMap

In addition to the general analytics described in Part A §3.1, MealMap sends information about your activity to Google Firebase Analytics so we can understand how the App is used. This includes content you create, such as:

  • That a venue was added, and the venue's name
  • That a dish was added to a venue, and the dish's name
  • Ratings you give to venues or dishes

We do not send your location data — including venue addresses, latitude, or longitude — to analytics, and we do not send the names of people you log meals for or your free-text notes. Content sent to analytics is processed by Google and is subject to Google's privacy policy. We do not intend for analytics to contain personally identifiable information. Because fields such as venue and dish names are free-text and entered at your discretion, we ask that you not enter personal information into them; if you do, that information may be included in analytics. You can delete any content you have entered at any time (see "Your saved content and iCloud sync" below and Part A §9).

A note on meal data

MealMap is a food and venue logging tool. We do not ask for, and the App is not designed to collect, health information — such as medical conditions, allergy profiles, or dietary restrictions — and we do not use your logged meals to infer anything about your health. Your meal log lives on your device and in your private iCloud account; the only meal-related data that reaches our analytics is what is described above.

Your saved content and iCloud sync

MealMap stores the content you create — including venue names and addresses, dishes, the names of people you log meals for, ratings, and free-text notes — on your device using Apple CoreData. This content is automatically backed up and synced to your personal iCloud account via Apple CloudKit.

This data is stored in your private iCloud database, associated with your Apple ID. Apart from the content sent to analytics as described above, Cedar Line Studios cannot access the contents of your private iCloud database. iCloud data is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy. You can delete this data by deleting it in the App or by managing your iCloud storage in your device Settings.

MealMap provides an in-app Delete All Data option (in Settings) that erases your saved venues, dishes, people, ratings, and notes from your device and your iCloud database.
